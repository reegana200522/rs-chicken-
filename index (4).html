    Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

    How can I place an order?

    Pick your items above, set the quantity, fill in your name and phone number, then press “Send Order on WhatsApp”.

    How are the prices calculated?

    Each item has a fixed price shown on the menu. The Order Summary adds up your items and updates the grand total as you go.

    Which payment methods are available?

    You can choose UPI / QR payment, cash, or pay at the shop.

    Can I order multiple food items together?

    Yes. Add as many items and quantities as you like before sending your order.

    Is my order confirmed immediately?

    Your order is sent to the shop on WhatsApp. The shop confirms it there.

    Where is the shop located?

    Shop #12, Ground Floor, Food Street Boulevard, Main Junction.

    Can I request special instructions?

    Yes. Mention them (e.g. extra spicy, less oil, pack separately) in your WhatsApp chat after sending the order.
